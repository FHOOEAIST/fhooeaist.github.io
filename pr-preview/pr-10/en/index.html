eHealth

IT support for integrated care ("eHealth") enables the definition, analysis and optimisation of suitable interfaces, adequate care processes and affordable business models for high-quality care and case management in healthcare. The goal of integrated care is the provision of comprehensive, coordinated and continuous services that ensure a seamless care process along the entire continuum of care. [after Hagen Kühn, Wissenschaftszentrum Berlin]

Machine Learning & Data Science

The field of Machine Learning deals with algorithms that are able to automatically find a domain-specific result based on patterns in data. This includes methods for the classification and identification of subjects in digital information, which are also able to continuously improve their own results as more data becomes available. The basic idea is not to explicitly teach algorithms how to solve a task, but to achieve this automatically using training data.

View 43 projects

Visual Computing

Visual Computing covers both the analysis and the generation of visual information. Computer Vision and Image Processing extract semantic information from image content, giving digital systems an ability comparable to human visual perception. Computer Graphics creates and renders digital content, and Mixed Reality combines information from the real world with digital data. Our work spans image acquisition, pre-processing, analysis and visualisation.
