## Airmate Motor Tests

AirMate is developing an innovative, electronically controlled rescue system designed to improve child safety. At its core is a safety-critical mechanism that must perform reliably under real-world conditions. To ensure consistent quality, AirMate combines advanced testing, high-resolution measurement data and data-driven evaluation methods to make every critical component as dependable as possible.
