## TecCOMFrame+

TecCOMFrame Plus is a European initiative shaping the future of technical communication education. Building on the original TecCOMFrame project, it updates academic competence requirements to reflect digitalization, artificial intelligence, and evolving professional roles. The project brings together universities and industry partners to support future-ready curricula, skills development, and career opportunities in technical communication.
