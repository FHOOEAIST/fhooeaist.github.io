## WTA

The WTA research project develops an intelligent prototype that combines AI, automated 3D reconstruction, Digital Twins and optimized workpiece carrier planning to transform quotation and production planning in the coating industry. Technical drawings and enquiry data are automatically analyzed, converted into usable 3D models and used for transparent, data-driven calculation and planning.
