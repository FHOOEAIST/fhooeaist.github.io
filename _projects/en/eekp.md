## eEKP

The eEKP project is further developing the digital version of the Parent-Child Health Record. Medical examinations and forms are being implemented in a structured manner as FHIR Questionnaires and mapped to the designated SVC interface. The goal is to make the relevant sections, parameters, and examination results of the Parent-Child Health Record available in a fully digital and standardized format, in accordance with the data collection concept.
