## Scalable Knowledge-Data Architecture for Founders

As part of the Technology Check 2026, we are collaborating with innoflow to explore how implicit knowledge — including that of founders, subject matter experts, and experienced professionals — can be translated into a quality-assured, role-based, and scalable digital knowledge architecture.

The focus is on modern technologies such as semantic search, RAG, vector databases, and AI agents. Using funding consulting as an example, the project is examining, among other things, a structured knowledge space, intelligent onboarding support, and automated funding news. The resulting architecture is intended to be transferable to other knowledge-intensive micro-enterprises.
