## AI-supported Generation of Presentations

The project is a research cooperation between Doka Österreich GmbH and the University of Applied Sciences Upper Austria exploring how artificial intelligence can support the automated creation of management presentations from financial data. The project develops and evaluates a proof-of-concept solution that combines AI-based reporting, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), expert feedback and learning approaches to transform financial tables into clear, presentation-ready insights.
