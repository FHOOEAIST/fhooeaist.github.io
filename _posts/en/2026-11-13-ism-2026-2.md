Martin and Mert had the opportunity to represent our research group at the 8th International Conference on Industry of the Future and Smart Manufacturing (ISM 2026), held in Padua and Venice from 11 to 13 November. They presented two current papers on applying large language models in manufacturing and process management: a process-anchored knowledge assistant for the maintenance of production machines, tested in a pilot at an injection molding company, and a pipeline that generates BPMN process models from natural-language requirements, reusing existing process templates in a traceable way and safeguarding the result with deterministic checks.

- M. Hanreich, G.A. Zwettler, K. Kloiber, J. Schönböck, J. Altmann – PAKA: A Process-Anchored Knowledge Assistant for Manufacturing Processes
- M. Dalkilic, A. Pointner, G.A. Zwettler – Template-Guided BPMN Process Generation with Traceable LLM Output and Deterministic Guardrails

We would like to thank all participants for the engaging discussions, the valuable feedback on our presentations, and the intensive professional exchange throughout the entire conference.
