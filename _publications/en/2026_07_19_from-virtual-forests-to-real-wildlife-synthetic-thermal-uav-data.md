This poster investigates the use of synthetic, non-radiometric thermal UAV imagery for training animal-detection models, presented as Article 68 in the SIGGRAPH 2026 Posters programme. By combining computer-graphics techniques with computer-vision methods, the work explores how simulated thermal drone footage of forests can support wildlife detection where real annotated thermal data is scarce.
