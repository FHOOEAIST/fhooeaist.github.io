The provision of customized orthopedic aids, such as prostheses and orthoses, is essential for treating congenital malformations, chronic diseases, and musculoskeletal injuries. Traditionally, these aids have been crafted manually by skilled technicians; however, additive manufacturing techniques and digital methodologies in the context of Industry 4.0 are revolutionizing this field. This work explores the automated customization of orthopedic aids using a two-step pipeline that leverages digital scans and structural analysis: the first step adapts orthopedic aids based on 3D scans of the patient's body, and the second step employs the Finite Element Method (FEM) to analyze structural integrity.
