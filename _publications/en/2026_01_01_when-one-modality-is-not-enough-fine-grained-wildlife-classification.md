This work studies multimodal sex and life-stage classification of red deer from aerial RGB-thermal drone video. Sex classification is challenging in thermal-only footage because antlers, a key visual cue, become nearly invisible in thermal imagery, whereas they remain clearly visible in RGB. We show that fusing RGB and thermal modalities yields the best classification results, demonstrating that relying on a single sensing modality is insufficient for reliable sex and life-stage classification of wildlife from aerial video.
