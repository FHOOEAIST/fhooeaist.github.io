eHealth

We develop software for connected, standards-based healthcare: interoperable solutions based on HL7 FHIR and IHE, process mining in hospitals and radiology, digitally supported integrated care and, increasingly, AI in clinical settings – together with partners from healthcare and industry.

Machine Learning & Data Science

We make data from industry, business and public administration usable: from knowledge bases and predictive maintenance in production and alarm management in critical infrastructure to large language models that relieve experts of text and learning tasks. Explainable, human-centred AI is a focus of its own.

Visual Computing

We work along the whole chain from image acquisition through analysis to visualisation – with a focus on drone-based wildlife monitoring in thermal and RGB imagery and on AI-supported quality inspection and 3D reconstruction in industry, complemented by mixed reality and synthetic training data.
